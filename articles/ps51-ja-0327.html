## SYNOPSIS
Modifies a certificate authority.

## SYNTAX

### SelfIssued

Set-AdfsCertificateAuthority [-SelfIssued] [-PassThru] [-WhatIf] [-Confirm] [<CommonParameters>]
```

 [<CommonParameters>]
